# Payment disputes and unrecognized charges

## Policy

Disputed or unauthorized charges are always escalated to the billing team. Agents do not promise an outcome or issue a refund on their own.

## Information to collect

Invoice number, charge date and amount, and the last four digits of the card only. Never collect full card numbers.
